About

The comic

Justin Fuller is a stand-up comedian based in Cleveland, Ohio.

He started doing comedy in 2021 and is now about five years in. His sets come down to three things: timing, confidence and material.

He plays clubs well beyond Cleveland. In May 2026 he came through Seattle and played Emerald City Comedy Club on Capitol Hill.
