The comic

Justin Fuller is a stand-up comedian from Erie, Pennsylvania, now working out of Cleveland, Ohio. He’s five years into a career he started in 2021.

He works rooms well past his home base. In May 2026 he took his act to Seattle, including a set at Emerald City Comedy Club on Capitol Hill.

He's the guy on stage in the "Support Your Local Stand-Up Comedian" shirt. Take the advice.

“He’s not up and coming. He’s here. If you see his name on a bill, go.”

André Graves · Photographer, friend of 40 years
